How the platform works

Platform overview

Market data, made readable

CryptoViz pulls together live prices, historical trends, volatility, correlation and market sentiment for the top cryptocurrencies into a single dashboard. The aim is straightforward: make the numbers easy to read, compare and reason about, without the usual clutter.

What you can explore

Live prices

Prices, market cap and 24-hour change for the leading coins, refreshed every few minutes throughout the day.

Normalized trends

Rebase several coins to a common starting point so you can compare how they move, independent of their price.

Volatility

Standard deviation of returns and maximum drawdown, so you can see how much movement sits behind each coin.

Correlation

A returns-based matrix that shows which assets tend to move together and which move in opposite directions.

Sentiment

News and Reddit headlines scored for tone, combined with the Crypto Fear & Greed Index into a single reading.

Top gainers

The strongest 24-hour performers, ranked and refreshed through the day so you can spot what is moving.

Where the data comes from

Market data

Prices, market cap and volume are scraped from CoinMarketCap, with the CoinGecko API as a fallback when the primary source is unavailable. Readings are recorded roughly every five minutes and kept for about a month - that rolling history is what powers the trend, volatility and correlation views.

Sentiment

Recent headlines from major crypto news sites and posts from r/CryptoCurrency are scored with the VADER model. Those scores are blended with the Fear & Greed Index - weighted 50% news, 30% social and 20% index - into one figure that updates alongside the price data.
